      How to read this report

      Each timeline query is retrieved against the full selected candidate index. Sliding-window features average the embeddings of overlapping windows; optional smoothing averages nearby queries. Segment captions use a normalized duration-weighted mean of the cached features, unless fresh isolated-cut encoding was requested.

      Numbers are cosine similarities, or temporal means of cosine similarities. They are not probabilities. Boundaries are heuristic acoustic changes; they do not separate simultaneous sound sources. Adjacent hierarchical clustering merges neighboring spans and may return fewer than the requested number to respect the minimum duration.
